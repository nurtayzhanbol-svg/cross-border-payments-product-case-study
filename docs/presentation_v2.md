# Small, regular remittances: where Wise's pricing model is weakest
Independent PM portfolio case study · World Bank RPW data (2011–2025) + public sources
*Not affiliated with Wise or the World Bank. Survey quotes, unweighted; not volumes.*

---

## 1. The market: cheaper, but uneven
- Median corridor cost 4.50% at USD 200 (latest four quarters)
- Surviving provider–corridor pairs fell 1.15 pp since 2016 (66% fell); only 60% survive
- Zero-fee quotes are cheaper all-in in 95% of corridors → the "no-fee trap" story was wrong
- Fixed fees make USD 200 relatively expensive

---

## 2. Wise already solved transparency
Mid-market rate · fee breakdown · comparison tool · scheduled transfers · wallet payout (some) · volume discount from £20k/month
→ The original "True Cost" idea duplicated Wise and was dropped.
Cash pickup: not found (NOT VERIFIED).

---

## 3. Wise in the survey (127 corridors)
| | USD 200 | USD 500 |
|---|---:|---:|
| Wise median total cost | 2.65% | 1.92% |
| Wise above 3% | 42.5% of corridors | 23.6% |
| Most quotes cheaper than Wise | 34 corridors | 26 |
Implied fixed fee ≈ USD 2.23 → ≈1.1 pp at USD 200. 11 corridors uncompetitive at USD 200 only.

---

## 4. Six options, scored on evidence first
Regular small senders **3.80** · Route repricing 3.25 · Cash pickup 3.10 · Wallets 3.00 · Platform for banks 2.60 · True Cost 2.30
Robust to equal weights and to removing feasibility. Cash pickup = biggest gap, least testable.

---

## 5. Hypothesis: reward regularity, not only volume
Recurring scheduled sends ≤ USD 500 to the same recipient get a reduced fixed fee in pilot corridors; saving shown upfront.
Risk: break-even needs about +35% to +66% retained transfers depending on unknown cost per transfer.
Invalidated if: few regular small senders, current prices already competitive, lift below break-even, price not the driver.

---

## 6. Test before building
Sender-level randomised pilot: control / −50% / −100% fixed fee, 3–5 corridors, 12 weeks
Primary: share of eligible senders with ≥3 transfers to the same recipient (all arms)
Guardrails: net revenue per sender, contribution, splitting, fraud/AML, complaints
≈4,100 senders per arm (60% → 63%) · Ship / iterate / stop thresholds pre-agreed
